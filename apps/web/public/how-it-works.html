The three steps

  1. Problem

    ThinkThrough restates your problem and confirms it with you before any research. No research runs until you say "yes, that's it".

  2. Smallest thing that solves it

    It checks what already exists, then proposes the smallest solution shape that plausibly solves the problem.

  3. Evidence

    Every claim has a source or is labelled inference or unknown. No claim without a source.
