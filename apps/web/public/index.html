How it works

Every case starts with the same three free steps. You can stop at any of them, and each one ends with at most five findings, what is still unknown, and one question.

See how it works

  1. Understand

    ThinkThrough restates the problem, who has it and what "solved" looks like, then loops until you say "yes, that's it". No research runs before you confirm.

  2. Already exists?

    It searches for products, open-source projects, scripts, templates and services that already solve your confirmed problem, and scores each against your scenario.

  3. Right-size

    It proposes the smallest solution shape that plausibly solves the problem, says what would justify stepping up, and asks you to choose.

Our honesty promise

  • No claim without a source, no number without a method — otherwise it is labelled inference or unknown.
  • Smallest thing first: the simpler shape is argued for before the bigger one.
  • It is critical of everyone — you, the internet, and itself. It says "I don't know", and it never flatters.
  • When the honest answer is "do not build", that is what it says: "you don't need a product — use X".
